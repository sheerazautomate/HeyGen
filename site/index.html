GOOD TO KNOW

A simpler way
to press play.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If someone has given you a compatible script.html, just choose it here and follow the steps. You do need a GitHub account for this GitHub-only pilot.

How long does a video take?

Usually a few minutes after your request is accepted. GitHub queues and renderer setup can add time. Check your request here; you don’t have to keep the page open.

Who can use the pilot?

Anyone can explore the site and gallery. Rendering is limited to accounts approved by the owner. Requests outside the pilot are declined automatically. Daily limits reset at midnight UTC.

Can I delete or keep my video private?

This pilot is public only. The request contains your source HTML, and the video is a public GitHub Release. Don’t include personal information or secrets. Contact the owner to request removal; public copies may remain elsewhere.
